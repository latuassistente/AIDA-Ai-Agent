# Human Handoff

AIDA escalates before failure.

## GREEN
Autonomous execution within explicit business rules.

## YELLOW
Approval required for exceptional or commercially sensitive actions, such as unusual discounts, non-standard terms or ambiguous commitments.

## RED
Human takeover for disputes, contract changes, legal-sensitive matters, high-impact decisions, unresolved identity/authorization or repeated uncertainty.

A handoff must preserve context so the human does not need to restart the conversation.
